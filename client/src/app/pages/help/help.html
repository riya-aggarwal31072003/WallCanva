How to use WallCanva

  1. Upload a clear photo of your room (JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB). Only upload photos you own.
  2. Click Select wall area, then click points around the wall. Click the red first point to close the shape.
  3. Pick a colour or pattern. Use the opacity slider and blend mode to adjust the look.
  4. Add more areas for a dual-tone wall. Click an area's chip to edit it, and drag the white points to reshape it.
  5. Use Undo/Redo (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) if you make a mistake.
  6. Drag the Before / After slider to compare with the original photo.
  7. Click Save design to keep it, or Download image for a PNG.
  8. Use New trial on My Designs to try another colour scheme on the same photo.

Colours may vary due to lighting, screen calibration and wall texture. Treat the preview as a guide, and check a physical sample before painting.
